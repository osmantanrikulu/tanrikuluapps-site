Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026 · Applies to the WashPro and OtoparkPro apps

Summary

Our apps keep your business data on your device first. Creating a cloud account is optional; if you create one, your data is backed up in an access-controlled area that belongs to your business alone. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

What data do we process?

  • Business records: licence plates, service and pricing details, payment and expense records, staff names. You create these and they belong to you.
  • Photos: vehicle check-in/check-out or before/after photos are stored on your device and deleted automatically at the end of the retention period you choose.
  • Account information (optional cloud): your email address, used only for sign-in and verification.
  • Subscription status: store subscriptions are verified through the App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat; we never see your payment details.

Where is data stored?

Local data stays on your device. If cloud backup is enabled, data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the European Union (Frankfurt) region, with per-business access rules (row level security). An internet connection is not required; syncing happens once you are back online.

Customer tracking links

In WashPro a business can give its customer a QR link showing the status of their vehicle. That link exposes only the plate of that vehicle, the job status and the estimated handover time; it cannot reach any other business data.

Your rights

You keep full control of your data: you can delete your records from inside the app and close your cloud account at any time. For questions and deletion requests: osmantanrikulu@gmail.com

Step-by-step instructions are on the account deletion page.

Changes

When this policy is updated the date on this page changes; significant changes are announced inside the app.
